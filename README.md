# PF2e Avatars - Battle forms for the Avatar spell
- Adds a compendium containing Battle form effects for the Avatar spell for each deity (when the data is available) and for each Animist Apparition that you can drag on your divine spellcaster.
- The appropriate Battle Form will be applied automatically based on your Deity when you cast the spell.
- For Animists, if you use the PF2E Dailies module to select your Attunements, the Battle Form matching your Primary Attunement will be applied automatically. If you are a Medium, you will be asked to choose from among your 2 primary Attunements. If you don't use PF2e Dailies, you will be asked to choose the Battle Form when you cast the spell.
- If you use the PF2e anachronism module, the PF2e deities effects will work in SF2e.
- If a deity has both a PF2e and a SF2e version, the avatar specific to the current system will be used.
- If you have the [PF2e Tokens: Character Gallery](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-tokens-character-gallery-foundry-vtt-code) and/or [PF2E Tokens: Myth and Magic](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-tokens-myth-and-magic-foundry-vtt-code) modules, the tokens from these modules will be used for the avatar transformation.
